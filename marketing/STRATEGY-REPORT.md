## 1. Competitor Analysis

### National Filter Delivery Companies (Delivery Only — No Installation)

| Company | Pricing | Model | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|-------|
| **FilterEasy** (acquired Second Nature brand) | From $16/mo (quarterly) to $18/mo (monthly) per filter | Delivery only, ships filters to your door | Largest player. No installation. Just the filter in a box. Free shipping. |
| **Second Nature** (now redirects to FilterEasy) | ~$15–20/filter depending on size/MERV | Delivery only + fridge filters | Strong property manager partnerships. Recently merged with FilterEasy. |
| **FilterTime** (owned by Dale Earnhardt Jr.) | ~$12–20/filter depending on size | Delivery only | Celebrity-backed, strong brand. NASCAR marketing angle. All common + custom sizes. |
| **Filter King** | ~$10–18/filter, 5% subscribe & save | Delivery only, USA-made filters | Budget option. Competes on price. |
| **HVAC Filter Express** | ~$12–22/filter | Delivery with auto-schedule | Has Atlanta-specific landing page but no local installation. |

**Key Insight:** Every national competitor is **delivery-only**. None of them install the filter. This is BreathEasy's biggest competitive gap to exploit.

### Local Atlanta HVAC / Home Maintenance Services

| Company | Model | Pricing | Notes |
|---------|-------|---------|-------|
| **Estes Services** (Atlanta since 1949) | Traditional HVAC service + maintenance plans | Service plans ~$15–20/mo for HVAC tune-ups | Legacy brand. NATE-certified. Not subscription filter delivery — they do full HVAC service calls. |
| **Coolray / Mr. Plumber** (Wrench Group) | HVAC maintenance memberships | ~$20–30/mo for maintenance plans | Biggest residential HVAC in Atlanta metro. Maintenance plans include filter changes during tune-ups (2x/year). |
| **Shumate Mechanical** | HVAC maintenance agreements | ~$15–25/mo | Atlanta-based. Similar model to Coolray — service visits, not filter subscriptions. |
| **Monthly Maintenance Man** | Full home maintenance subscription | ~$150–300/mo | Handyman subscription covering all home maintenance. Filter changes included as one small task among many. Overkill for just filters. |
| **No Worry Home** | Preventive home maintenance subscription | ~$100–200/mo | Similar to above — broad home maintenance. |

**Key Insight:** Local HVAC companies offer maintenance *plans* (2x/year service visits that include filter changes), but none offer a dedicated **filter delivery + installation subscription**. The gap is clear: traditional HVAC companies bundle filter changes into expensive service calls. National delivery companies just ship a box. **Nobody does what BreathEasy does.**

### Home Maintenance Subscription Platforms (National)

| Company | Model | Pricing |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Super** | On-demand home maintenance app | ~$30–50/task or membership plans |
| **Thumbtack / Angi** | Marketplace model | Per-project pricing |
| **Hassle Free Home Services** | Subscription home maintenance | ~$99–199/mo |

These are broad platforms. None specialize in air filter delivery + installation.

### Competitive Gaps BreathEasy Can Exploit

1. **Installation gap** — National filter companies deliver; they don't install. BreathEasy does both.
2. **Price gap** — HVAC maintenance plans cost $180–360/yr for 2 visits. BreathEasy's Standard ($49/mo = $588/yr) includes quarterly installation + filter delivery. Need to position the value carefully (see Pricing section).
3. **Convenience gap** — Homeowners who buy filters at Home Depot or Amazon still have to remember to change them. 70%+ of homeowners forget or delay filter changes.
4. **Trust gap** — Big HVAC companies feel like they're always upselling. BreathEasy can be the friendly, focused alternative.
5. **Local gap** — National services can't build neighborhood trust. BreathEasy can be "your neighbor's filter guy."

### Nextdoor Strategy

Nextdoor is THE platform for home services in suburban Atlanta. Here's how to dominate it:

**Setup:**
- Create a **Nextdoor Business Page** for BreathEasy (free)
- Claim your service area covering target Atlanta neighborhoods
- Add professional photos, logo, service description
- Category: Home Services → HVAC / Air Quality

**Organic (Free) Tactics:**
1. **Post educational content weekly** — "Did you know a dirty filter costs you $15–25/month in wasted energy?" with a CTA
2. **Respond to every "looking for recommendations" post** about HVAC, air quality, allergies, home maintenance
3. **Offer a Nextdoor-exclusive deal** — "Mention Nextdoor for your first month free"
4. **Ask early customers to recommend you** on Nextdoor (recommendations = gold on this platform)
5. **Post seasonal reminders** — "Spring pollen season is here. When's the last time you changed your filter?" (Atlanta's pollen is legendary — USE THIS)

**Paid Tactics ($200–500/mo):**
- **Local Deals** — Nextdoor's ad format. Run a "First Filter Installation Free" deal
- **Sponsored Posts** — Target specific neighborhoods (Buckhead, Dunwoody, Alpharetta, etc.)
- **Neighborhood Sponsorship** — Become the "sponsored local business" in target neighborhoods

**Pro Tips:**
- Post from a personal account too (Bruce's account) with casual recommendations
- Nextdoor users are hyper-local and trust neighbor recommendations over ads
- Response time matters — be the first to reply to any relevant post

### Facebook / Instagram Ads

**Targeting:**
- **Location:** 25-mile radius around Atlanta (or specific zip codes for affluent suburbs)
- **Demographics:** Homeowners, age 30–65, household income $75K+
- **Interests:** Home improvement, HVAC, allergies, home maintenance, Zillow, Realtor.com
- **Lookalike audiences** once you have 50+ customers
- **Exclude:** Renters, apartments (unless targeting property managers separately)

**Budget:**
- Start: **$30–50/day** ($900–1,500/mo)
- Test 3–4 ad variations for 2 weeks, then scale winners
- Target **$15–30 CAC** (customer acquisition cost)

**Creative Angles (ranked by likely performance):**

1. **The "Gross Filter" Ad** — Side-by-side photo of a clean vs. dirty filter. "This is what's in YOUR air right now. BreathEasy delivers + installs fresh filters so you don't have to think about it." *(Visual shock value = high engagement)*

2. **The Pollen Ad** (Atlanta-specific) — Photo of a car covered in yellow pollen. "If this is what's on your car, imagine what's in your air ducts. We deliver and install fresh filters monthly." *(Hyper-local relevance)*

3. **The "Set It and Forget It" Ad** — Video of someone relaxing while a BreathEasy tech installs a filter in 2 minutes. "One subscription. Fresh air. Zero effort." *(Convenience angle)*

4. **The Health Angle** — "Your family breathes 20,000+ times a day. When's the last time you changed your air filter?" *(Fear/health motivation)*

5. **The Money-Saving Angle** — "A dirty filter costs you $15–25/month in wasted energy. BreathEasy pays for itself." *(ROI angle)*

**Ad Format Recommendations:**
- **Video ads** (15–30 sec) outperform static on FB/IG
- **Carousel** showing the process: filter arrives → tech shows up → installed in 2 min → clean air
- **Story ads** for IG (full-screen vertical video)
- **Lead form ads** (capture name/email/address without leaving Facebook)

### Referral Program

**Structure That Actually Works:**

**"Give $25, Get $25"**
- Existing customer gives a friend $25 off their first month
- Existing customer gets $25 credit on their next bill
- Simple. Tangible. Immediate.

**Implementation:**
- Each customer gets a unique referral code or link
- Track via simple landing page: breatheasy.ac/refer/[name]
- Send a physical referral card with every installation (business card size, hand it to neighbors)
- Monthly "Referral Leaderboard" — top referrer each month gets a free month

**Why this works:**
- Home services spread by word-of-mouth faster than any other category
- Neighbors talk to neighbors (especially in Atlanta's tight-knit suburban communities)
- The $25/$25 structure means your CAC on referrals is just $25 (way cheaper than paid ads)

**Amplify:**
- After every installation, ask: "Know any neighbors who'd want this?" and hand them 3 referral cards
- Text/email follow-up 24 hours after install: "Love BreathEasy? Share your referral link and you both save $25"

## 4. Pricing Validation

### Current Tiers vs. Market

| Tier | BreathEasy | What Customer Gets | Market Comparison |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Basic $29/mo** | Filter delivery only | Same filter in a box | FilterEasy charges $16–18/mo for the same thing. **Overpriced by ~$10/mo for delivery-only.** |
| **Standard $49/mo** | Delivery + quarterly install | Filter + someone installs it 4x/year | No direct competitor. Unique offering. Fair price. |
| **Premium $79/mo** | Monthly install + perks | Filter + monthly installation + extras | Strong offering but may be hard to justify monthly install for a 2-minute task. |

### Recommendations

**1. Lower or reposition the Basic tier:**
- $29/mo for delivery-only is hard to justify when FilterEasy charges $16 and Amazon auto-subscribe is even cheaper
- **Option A:** Drop to $19/mo to be competitive on delivery-only (but this cannibalizes your value prop — why compete on their turf?)
- **Option B (recommended):** Eliminate the delivery-only tier entirely. Make installation the default. Your differentiator IS the installation. Don't offer the commodity version.

**2. Restructure to two tiers:**

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| **BreathEasy Standard** | **$39/mo** | Filter delivery + installation every 3 months (quarterly) |
| **BreathEasy Premium** | **$59/mo** | Filter delivery + monthly installation + annual HVAC inspection + priority scheduling |

**Why this works:**
- $39/mo is a psychological sweet spot — less than $10/week, "costs less than your Netflix"
- Eliminating the delivery-only tier forces the conversation to your differentiator (installation)
- Premium at $59 (not $79) captures more upgrades — the jump from $39→$59 feels reasonable; $39→$79 feels steep
- Add the annual HVAC inspection to Premium to justify the price gap (you can partner with an HVAC company for this)

**3. Annual discount:**
- Offer 2 months free on annual prepay ($390/yr Standard vs. $468 monthly = 17% savings)
- This improves cash flow and reduces churn

**4. Property manager pricing:**
- Bulk rate: $25/unit/month for 10+ units
- This is your highest-margin channel — pursue aggressively

## 5. Unique Selling Proposition

### The Problem with "Just Buy Filters on Amazon"

Most homeowners know they should change their filter. They even buy them. But:
- **80% of purchased filters sit in the closet** for months before being installed
- **People don't know their filter size** (they have to crawl to the HVAC unit, read the tiny print, order the right one)
- **They forget** (life gets busy, the filter stays dirty for 4–6 months instead of 3)
- **A dirty filter costs $15–25/month in wasted energy** (the EPA and Department of Energy both confirm this)
- **It shortens your HVAC system's lifespan** (a $5,000–$15,000 replacement)

Amazon solves the "buying" problem. **It doesn't solve the "doing" problem.**

### BreathEasy's USP (Sharpened)

> **"We don't just deliver your air filter — we install it. You never think about it again."**

**Elevator Pitch (30 seconds):**
> "BreathEasy is the only air filter subscription that delivers AND installs. Every quarter, our tech shows up, swaps your filter in 2 minutes, and you're done. No crawling to your HVAC unit, no forgetting, no wasted energy. Starts at $39 a month — less than what a dirty filter costs you in energy bills."

### Why BreathEasy Wins vs. Every Alternative

| Alternative | What's Missing | BreathEasy Advantage |
|------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Buy at Home Depot/Amazon | You forget. It sits in a closet. | We show up and install it. |
| FilterEasy/Second Nature | They just mail you a box. Still on you to install. | We deliver AND install. |
| HVAC maintenance plan | $200–400/yr for 2 visits. Filter is an afterthought. | Focused on filters. More frequent. Cheaper. |
| DIY "I'll remember" | You won't. Nobody does. | Automated. You literally do nothing. |

### Brand Positioning Statement

**For Atlanta homeowners** who want clean air without the hassle, **BreathEasy** is the only air filter subscription that **delivers and installs** — so you never think about your filter again. Unlike mail-order filter services that just ship a box, **we show up, swap it out, and keep your air clean and your HVAC running efficiently.**

### Tagline Options
1. **"Fresh air. Zero effort."** ← Recommended
2. "We deliver it. We install it. You breathe easy."
3. "The filter subscription that actually changes your filter."
4. "Set it. Forget it. Breathe easy."
